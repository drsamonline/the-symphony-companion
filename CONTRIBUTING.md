## Reporting a problem

Open an [issue](../../issues) and include:

- what you expected and what happened,
- the device and browser (e.g. "iPhone, Safari 18" or "Windows, Chrome 130"),
- whether JavaScript was enabled or blocked,
- a screenshot if you can.

Accessibility, offline and no-JavaScript issues are especially welcome — the app is
meant to work in all three.

## Suggesting a change

Small fixes (typos, layout, wording) are fine as a pull request. For anything
larger — a new screen, a change to the content of a chapter card, a restyle — please
open an issue first so we can agree the approach.

## Style

- One self-contained `index.html`; no frameworks, no build step, no external
  requests.
- The core navigation must keep working with **JavaScript disabled**.
- Keep the accessibility and privacy promises intact: no accounts, no tracking, no
  network calls.
- Match the existing teal palette and the book's typography.

## What not to change without the author's agreement

- The chapter text, ideas and quotations (they come from the book).
- The licence, the attribution, or the wellbeing disclaimer.
